About the Course

Offered by The GRAPH Courses (University of Geneva, Switzerland)

Generative AI for Work & Research: No-Code AI Tools for Responsible Task Automation

An 8-week bootcamp-style live cohort course designed to equip researchers and professionals with practical, no-code AI tools. The curriculum combines academic rigor with practical workflows for task automation, automated literature analysis, custom chatbot design, and statistical data processing.

Live Workshops

Interactive group exercises, real-time code-alongs, and whole-class teaching.

Applied Prework

~4 hours per week of hands-on tasks, prompt engineering, and literature extraction.

Responsible AI Practice

Focus on verifying statistical outputs, mitigating hallucinations, context windows, and privacy.

Academic Backing

Non-profit program backed by University of Geneva, WHO, SSPH+, and global partners.
